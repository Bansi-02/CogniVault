MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Acme Corp, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 123 Innovation Drive, San Francisco, CA 94107 ("Client"), and Globex Dynamics Ltd., a limited company organized under the laws of the United Kingdom with its principal place of business at 45 Canary Wharf, London E14 5AB ("Provider"). Client and Provider may be referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, Client desires to engage Provider to perform certain software development, data analytics, and cloud hosting services; and
WHEREAS, Provider desires to perform such services for Client, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises herein contained, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public, proprietary information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally, in writing, or by inspection of tangible objects, which is designated as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or some similar designation, or which should reasonably be understood to be confidential.
1.2 "Deliverables" means all software, source code, object code, documentation, reports, algorithms, and other materials developed or provided by Provider to Client under any Statement of Work (SOW).
1.3 "Services" means the consulting, development, maintenance, and cloud hosting services to be performed by Provider as described in an SOW.

2. SERVICES AND STATEMENTS OF WORK
2.1 Provision of Services. Provider shall perform the Services and deliver the Deliverables as specified in one or more mutually agreed upon Statements of Work ("SOW"). Each SOW shall be governed by this Agreement.
2.2 Vendor Risk and Subcontracting. Provider shall not subcontract any portion of the Services to third-party vendors without Client's prior written consent. Provider remains fully liable for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors. All subcontractors must be vetted through Client's internal vendor risk screening protocols.

3. FEES, BILLING, AND PAYMENT
3.1 Fees. Client shall pay Provider the fees set forth in each SOW. Unless otherwise specified, all fees are in United States Dollars (USD).
3.2 Invoicing. Provider shall submit invoices to Client on a monthly basis. All invoices shall include a detailed line-item breakdown of hours worked, services rendered, and expenses incurred.
3.3 Payment Terms. Client shall pay all undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a valid invoice.
3.4 Audit and Fraud Prevention. Client reserves the right, upon ten (10) days written notice, to audit Provider's billing records and metadata logs to verify the accuracy of invoices and detect potential billing fraud or anomalies.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PRIVILEGE
4.1 Non-Disclosure. The Receiving Party shall not use any Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party for any purpose other than exercising its rights or fulfilling its obligations under this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall restrict access to Confidential Information to its employees and contractors who need to know such information.
4.2 Attorney-Client Privilege. Certain communications and documents shared under this Agreement may be subject to attorney-client privilege or the attorney work-product doctrine. Neither Party waives any such privilege by disclosing information pursuant to this Agreement.

5. DATA PRIVACY AND COMPLIANCE (GDPR & CCPA)
5.1 Compliance. Both Parties shall comply with all applicable data protection and privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA").
5.2 Data Processing. To the extent Provider processes any Personal Data on behalf of Client, such processing shall be governed by the Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") attached hereto as Exhibit A. Provider warrants that it maintains SOC 2 Type II compliance and ISO 27001 certification.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
6.1 Ownership of Deliverables. Upon full payment of all fees due under the applicable SOW, Client shall own all right, title, and interest in and to the Deliverables, including all intellectual property rights therein.
6.2 Provider Background IP. Provider retains all rights to any pre-existing software, tools, or methodologies ("Background IP") used in the creation of the Deliverables. Provider grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use the Background IP solely to the extent necessary to use the Deliverables.

7. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY
7.1 Indemnification. Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from and against any third-party claims, losses, or damages arising out of (a) Provider's breach of Section 4 (Confidentiality) or Section 5 (Data Privacy); or (b) any claim that the Deliverables infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.
7.2 Limitation of Liability. EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY, INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE, NEITHER PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL EXCEED THE TOTAL AMOUNTS PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT TO PROVIDER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION
8.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.
8.2 Termination for Cause. Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other Party materially breaches any term and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice.
8.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Provider shall immediately cease all Services, return all Client Confidential Information, and transfer all completed and partially completed Deliverables to Client.

9. GENERAL PROVISIONS
9.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
9.2 Corporate Structure. Provider represents that it is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Globex International Group. Provider shall notify Client of any changes in beneficial ownership within 15 days.
