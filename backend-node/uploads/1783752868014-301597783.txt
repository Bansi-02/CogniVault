Acme Corp - Financial Performance Report 2023
This document summarizes the monthly gross revenue generated by Acme Corp for the fiscal year 2023.

Executive Summary
Acme Corp experienced steady growth throughout the first half of the year, followed by accelerated performance in Q3 due to the launch of our new enterprise software suite. Q4 saw typical seasonal dips but remained strong overall.

Monthly Revenue Breakdown
January 2023: We started the year strong with $120,500 in gross revenue.
February 2023: Slight increase to $124,200.
March 2023: End of Q1 closed at $128,900.
April 2023: Q2 began with $132,400.
May 2023: Continued steady growth at $136,800.
June 2023: Strong finish for H1 at $141,500.
July 2023: Summer slump hit slightly, down to $138,200.
August 2023: Rebounded to $145,900.
September 2023: Enterprise product launch drove revenue up to $165,300.
October 2023: Sustained momentum at $172,100.
November 2023: Reached an all-time high of $185,400.
December 2023: Seasonal dip brought the year to a close at $178,000.
Forward Outlook
We anticipate continued growth in 2024 as we expand into the European market. Our predictive models suggest a baseline of $200k+ monthly recurring revenue by mid-2024.
